# Default visual language

A calm, precise interface: neutral surfaces, restrained purple signals, readable
density and clear actions. Avoid decorative dashboard filler, cinematic HUDs,
glow, ambient motion and ornamental cards. Product identity comes from relevant
content and branding, not effects. Project design always wins.

Treat these as role tokens, not automatic contrast compliance. Verify rendered
text/focus/disabled contrast at actual sizes. Grays dominate; purple marks primary
action/location/focus, green confirmed success, amber warning/pending, red errors
and destructive consequences. Always pair status with text/shape.

Use Geist Sans for UI and Geist Mono for identifiers/comparable technical values
when fonts are available; avoid adding dependencies without need. Compact
sentence-case headings, restrained uppercase eyebrows/table headers, readable body
copy. Typical radii: controls 6–8 px, panels/dialogs 9–12 px. Fine 1 px borders,
shallow sparse shadows, opaque readable surfaces, regular aligned spacing.
Use consistent simple line icons from the project's icon system.

Light/dark are equally considered. Preserve semantic contrast in both; switching
theme must not lose unsaved work or navigation. Keep motion brief and functional,
respect reduced motion, and never delay essential feedback. No looping decoration.

Adapt layouts rather than shrink screenshots. On narrow screens stack forms,
headings and actions; preserve critical comparison data and access to navigation.
Dialogs retain close/cancel/commit and logical focus. Inspect wrapping, clipping,
long content, keyboard focus, accessible names and touch targets at real sizes.
